# Study 2 Summary: PainRelief Tablets, Multi-Site Acute Pain Trial

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. Invented for a reference implementation. The organisation,
> sites, participants and results do not exist.

## Overview

An independent, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial of PainRelief
Tablets in adults with acute headache pain, run across five clinical sites in five
countries by the Meridian Clinical Research Network. The study team had no
investigators in common with any other published trial of this product. The
manufacturer funded the study but had no role in its design, conduct or analysis.

## Design

- Participants: 300 adults (150 treatment, 150 placebo), across five sites.
- One of the five sites was in India and enrolled 60 participants (30 treatment,
  30 placebo), recruited from the local population.
- Single dose, taken at the onset of a headache of moderate or greater intensity.
- Pain intensity was recorded by each participant at three time points after dosing:
  30 minutes, 60 minutes and 120 minutes.
- Primary endpoint: the proportion of participants reporting meaningful pain relief.

## Results

Proportion of participants reporting meaningful pain relief, all sites combined:

| Time after dose | PainRelief Tablets | Placebo |
|-----------------|--------------------|---------|
| 30 minutes      | 55%                | 29%     |
| 60 minutes      | 72%                | 36%     |
| 120 minutes     | 79%                | 39%     |

The difference between treatment and placebo was statistically significant at every
measured time point. Results at the India site were consistent with the combined
results (relief at 30 minutes: 53% with treatment against 27% with placebo).

## Limitations

Pain was not measured before 30 minutes after dosing. The trial therefore provides no
data on the effect of the tablets at any time earlier than 30 minutes, and it cannot
support statements about the onset of relief within that window.

## Conclusion

PainRelief Tablets provided significantly greater pain relief than placebo from 30
minutes after dosing, sustained to 120 minutes, including in the Indian site
population.
